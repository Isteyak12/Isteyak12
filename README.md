## 👋 About me

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://media4.giphy.com/media/bGgsc5mWoryfgKBx1u/200w.gif?cid=6c09b952hk36t76podqoceh464ouz1vbptyqlgu814ayfn7t&ep=v1_gifs_search&rid=200w.gif&ct=g" width="260" alt="Animated Character" />
</p>

I'm **Isteyak**, currently working on **machine vision system inspections**, building on earlier research at the **GeoVision Lab**, where I trained **Siamese Attention U-Net** models for satellite change detection. I love computer vision, deep learning and creative problem-solving, and lately I've been pointing that toolkit at **robotics**.

- 🔭 Working on **industrial computer vision inspection systems**
- 🌱 Learning **geospatial deep learning**, **TensorRT inference** and **ROS 2**
- 👯 Open to collaborating on **computer vision / AI** projects
- 💬 Ask me about **Python, computer vision and full-stack web dev**
- ⚡ Fun fact: I've trained models on everything from satellite imagery to posture-tracking webcams
